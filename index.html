From Start to Star: Odisha's SDG Journey

Odisha's composite score on NITI Aayog's SDG India Index rose 15 points between 2018 and 2023-24, from 51 to 66, moving it from “Performer” to “Front Runner” and making it one of India's fastest gainers. Reading all 16 goals across four editions shows the progress is uneven: gender equality and clean water run against the headline score.

Data: NITI Aayog SDG India Index, four editions

Odisha's Potato Paradox

Odisha planted 172% more land with potatoes between 2009-10 and 2023-24, and grew 139% more of them. Yield per hectare fell 12% over the same period. Fourteen years of production data, read against a state that needs 10.21 lakh tonnes a year and grows 4.28 — which is why a supply crunch in West Bengal moves prices in Bhubaneswar.

Data: Directorate of Agriculture & Food Production, Odisha; Indiastat; NSSO

The Economist called the US economy “the envy of the world” weeks before the election. Voters disagreed. Nine indicators set side by side across two administrations show where the aggregates and the experience came apart: GDP up $2 trillion a year and market wealth up 121%, against inflation of 20.5% and real wages down 2.4%.

Has Indian Tax System Become More Pro-Poor?

Direct taxes reached 57% of India's total tax revenue in FY24, the highest share in fourteen years and a ten-point rise in four. It matters because the other 43% is indirect tax, which falls on everyone who buys anything — including the poor.

Flaming Greens

Odisha recorded 1.39 lakh forest fire detections between November 2019 and June 2024 — more than any other Indian state, ahead of Madhya Pradesh's 1.23 lakh and Chhattisgarh's 1.09 lakh. India's national trend spikes in 2020-21 then declines steadily. Odisha's does not: a surge past 51,000, a dip, a second rise, then a 37% fall.

Data: Forest Survey of India; Odisha State Forest Department
