    For readers outside India

    A quick guide to the newspapers, institutions and terms on this page.

    Newspapers and broadcasters

    The Times of India
    India’s biggest English-language daily, selling about 1.8 million copies a day. It was founded in Bombay, now Mumbai, in 1838.
    The Hindu
    A national English-language daily founded in Madras, now Chennai, in 1878, and often described as one of India’s newspapers of record.
    The Indian Express
    A national daily founded in 1932. It led the Panama Papers investigation in India.
    Republic Media Network
    The company behind Republic TV, an English-language news channel launched in 2017, with sister channels in Hindi and other Indian languages.
    Network18 and Firstpost
    Network18 is a media group owned by Reliance Industries. Firstpost is its news website.

    Universities

    Jadavpur University
    A public university in Kolkata, ranked first among India’s state universities in the government’s 2025 national rankings.
    Symbiosis Institute of Media and Communication
    The media school of Symbiosis International University, a private university in Pune.

    Public bodies and data sources

    Association for Democratic Reforms (ADR)
    A non-partisan nonprofit, set up in 1999 by professors at the Indian Institute of Management Ahmedabad. It analyses the sworn declarations, or affidavits, that election candidates must file about their assets, education and criminal cases.
    Union Public Service Commission (UPSC)
    Runs the exam that selects India’s senior civil servants. More than half a million people sit its first stage each year.
    SBI Ecowrap
    Economic research published by the State Bank of India, the country’s largest bank.
    Forest Survey of India
    A government agency that maps the country’s forests and sends out forest fire alerts based on satellite data.

    Places, politics and words

    Lakh and crore
    Indian units for large numbers. A lakh is 100,000 and a crore is 10 million, so ₹1 crore is roughly £80,000 or $100,000. A crorepati is someone worth a crore or more.
    Lok Sabha
    The elected lower house of India’s parliament, comparable to the House of Commons or the House of Representatives. Each state also elects its own assembly, and general elections are held in phases over several weeks.
    BJP and JMM
    The Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) leads India’s national government. The Jharkhand Mukti Morcha (JMM) is a regional party in the state of Jharkhand.
    Odisha and Jharkhand
    Neighbouring states in eastern India, home to more than 40 million and more than 30 million people.
    Noida
    A city on Delhi’s south-eastern edge, part of the capital region and home to many national newsrooms.
    Sub-editor
    The British and Indian term for a copy editor.
    Statoistics
    The Times of India’s data column.
    IPL
    The Indian Premier League, India’s top professional cricket competition, played in the short Twenty20 format.
